About

Varia Aves builds techno in real time on a modular system. No backing tracks, no prepared arrangements.

Patterns start small, grow, mutate and collide while the room is dancing. The first minutes lay down a pulse; by minute forty the synth lines take turns nobody planned, the artist included. The sound sits between hypnotic and playful: driving rhythm underneath, curious, bending melodies on top.

Based in Berlin, Varia Aves has played Sisyphos, ://about blank and ANDEREWELT. Festival, and brings the same open-ended approach to every floor, from a sunrise slot to the peak of the night.

FAQ

Good to know
How do I book Varia Aves?

Send an email to varia.aves@gmail.com with the date, the venue or festival, the stage and the slot you have in mind. You'll get an answer within a few days.

What does a booking cost?

The fee depends on the event, the set length and travel. Send a short request with the details and you'll get a clear offer, no back and forth.

Is it really all improvised?

Yes. There are no backing tracks and no prepared arrangements. Every set is built live on the modular system and the TR-1000, and reacts to the room.

How long can you play?

Anything from 60 minutes up. The sets are at their best with time to build, so 90 minutes or longer, including sunrise and late-night slots.

What do you need on site?

A sturdy table, power, a stereo line into the mixer and a booth monitor. A short tech rider comes with every booking.

Do you play outside Berlin?

Yes. Varia Aves is based in Berlin and plays clubs and festivals across Europe.
